FAQs
How fast can I get the loan?

Personal and business loans can be sanctioned in a few days once documents are complete. Housing loans and LAP need property checks, so they usually take longer.

Does checking eligibility affect my credit score?

No. Talking to us and checking eligibility does not affect your score. A lender checks your credit only when you formally apply.

Can I apply with a low CIBIL score?

Yes, you can enquire. We'll suggest lenders and options suited to your profile and explain how to improve your score.

Which lenders do you work with?

We work with multiple banks and NBFCs so we can compare offers and find the right fit for you.
